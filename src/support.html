What gifts pay for

  • The collection: finding, checking and adding books and church records. Fixing errors when they're found.
  • Search: every question and every new book is read by an AI search service. It charges by the amount of text, so the cost grows with use.
  • The server: the computer that runs the library around the clock.

How to give

  • Buy Me a Coffee: monthly or once. Pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. No account needed.
  • GitHub Sponsors: monthly or once by card. You need a GitHub account.
  • Bitcoin: send over Lightning to mattm4444@strike.me (paste it into your wallet's send field).

The library is not yet a registered charity and has no fiscal sponsor. Gifts go to Matt Miller, its founder. They are not tax-deductible. It is not a ministry of any church. Giving is never needed to use it.
