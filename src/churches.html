For churches, denominations and organizations

We put your constitution, minutes, reports and papers, public or confidential, beside about 7,700 Christian books and the records of more than 80 denominations. Your people study them from Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Grok.

Write to us

Ask how a rule changed, what an assembly or board decided, or for every report on a subject, and it answers from the documents themselves. There's nothing to install: they add one address to the app they already use.

Private papers stay private

You decide who reads each document: anyone, your members, or only the officers you name.

  • No one's questions are kept. The connector doesn't log what anyone searches for.
  • Never used to train AI, never sold. Voyage AI, which ranks the passages, deletes them straight away and doesn't train on them.
  • Encrypted on the way. Every request travels encrypted between the AI app and our server.
  • Check it yourself. Our code, security review and privacy notes are public.
  • Yours to take back. Ask, and we take your documents out and delete our copy.

A passage shown in someone's AI app stays in that conversation under the app's own terms, so open confidential papers only to people you'd trust with a printed copy.

Every quotation can be checked

Quotations are word for word, with the page and a link to the source. When your documents don't say, it tells you so. Our test results are public, misses and all.

What it costs

The public-domain books stay free for everyone. You pay only for our work on your documents: loading them, adding each new edition or set of minutes, and your own address. We'll build a working version on yours first, at no cost.
