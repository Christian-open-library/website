For churches

We put your church's constitution, minutes and reports beside the records of every body you came from. Your people study them from Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Grok.

Write to us

Ask how a rule changed, what an assembly decided, or for every report on a subject, and it answers from the documents themselves. There's nothing to install: they add one address to the app they already use.

Every quotation can be checked

Quotations are word for word, with the page and a link to the source. When your documents don't say, it tells you so. Our test results are public, misses and all.

Who sees what

We don't keep what anyone asks. Papers you don't publish can be kept to the people you choose.

What it costs

The public-domain books stay free for everyone. A church pays only for our work on its own documents: loading them, adding each new edition after your assembly or synod, and an address of your own. We'll build a working version on yours first, at no cost.
