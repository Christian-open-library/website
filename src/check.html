Checking an answer

How to check it

Don't take the library on trust. This is how to check it.

  • Quotations are word for word from the library's copy

    The connector returns each passage exactly as it stands in the library's copy, with the author and title, and the year and page where the source gives them. The copies are not perfect: scanned books have misread letters, and some texts have no year or page numbers.

  • Every quotation can be traced

    Each result carries a reference (such as P1234500017) and a link to where the library's copy came from. Results from Internet Archive scans link to the page itself. If an answer quotes an author without a reference and a link, ask your AI app where the words came from.

  • Your AI app writes the answer

    The connector finds and quotes. Your AI app (Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini) reads the passages and writes the answer, and it can summarize badly or quote from memory. Check a quotation against its source before you print or preach it.

  • It says when it has nothing

    When the library doesn't have a book, or has nothing close to what was asked, the connector says so and tells the AI not to fill the gap from memory. Each result is marked as an exact, close, partial or loose match.

  • Scans are marked

    Every passage from a scanned book is marked as uncorrected OCR, which means the computer's reading of the page may contain wrong letters.

  • Every book names its source

    Each file records where its text came from and its license. Only public-domain and openly licensed works are included.

  • The library doesn't modernize

    It doesn't modernize, correct or abridge the texts it copies. Some sources had already edited their editions; each file names its source, so you can see which edition you are reading.

  • It is measured

    How well it finds things is tested on a fixed set of questions, and the results are published, misses included.

  • Corrections are public

    Errors that are found and fixed are listed in the corrections log.

  • You can see where your question goes

    The connector keeps no copy of your conversations or documents and doesn't log what you search for. What happens to your question names every party that sees it.

  • Open code, reviewed with AI

    The code is public, and so is its security review. The review was carried out with Claude, Anthropic's AI. No outside firm has audited it.

  • One person, no ads

    A personal project curated by Matt Miller. It has no ads, and no church or denomination directs or endorses it.
