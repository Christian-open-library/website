What the answers mean

  • Found word for word. The words are in the book named, as you gave them. Spelling, capitals and punctuation aren't compared.
  • Found, but worded differently. The quotation has drifted as it was passed around. Use the real wording.
  • Found, but not by them. The words are in the library, but not in anything it has by the person they're credited to. It names the earliest book it has with them.
  • Not found. No book in the library has the words or anything close to them.

What it can't tell you

  • “Not found” doesn't mean fake. The library holds about 6,400 works, not everything ever written. Most books from after 1930, such as C. S. Lewis's, are still under copyright and aren't in it.
  • The earliest book it has isn't always the source. A saying can be older than any book the library holds.
  • Scanned books have misread letters. Where the text was read from a scan, open the printed page before you use the words.

Privacy

The words you check go to the library's server and to Voyage AI, which helps find reworded quotations. Cloudflare carries them. Neither the server nor Voyage keeps them. A share link carries the quotation inside the link itself, after the #, so this website never receives it. Your IP address is used only to count checks for the daily limit, as a scrambled code that changes every day and is deleted the next day. Details.
