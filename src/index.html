What's in it

  • Theology and devotion The Church Fathers, the Reformers, the Puritans, Edwards, the creeds and confessions.
  • Church records Constitutions, disciplines, digests and minutes of more than 80 denominations.
  • Commentaries Calvin, Matthew Henry, Gill, Poole, Keil and Delitzsch, with Greek and Hebrew lexicons.
  • Sermons Spurgeon, Whitefield, M'Cheyne, Chalmers, and Simeon's 2,500 outlines.
  • Journals and reference The Princeton Review (1831–1929), Bible translations, classic Bible dictionaries.
  • Poetry, fiction and philosophy Herbert, Milton, Donne, George MacDonald, Chesterton, Plato, Aristotle.

Every book is the published work of a human author or translator. None of it is written or translated by AI.

Your own church

Name your church, and the search keeps to its records and those of the bodies it came from: PCA, OPC, PC(USA), EPC, ARP, SBC, UMC, LCMS, ELCA, Episcopal, ACNA, Catholic, Orthodox and more.

I'm in the PCA. What did our Book of Church Order say about the qualifications of ruling elders?

Most are editions from before 1931, so your church's rules today may differ.

Things to ask

  • What did Matthew Henry say about Romans 8:28?
  • Find where a Puritan writes about assurance of salvation.
  • Which older writers quoted Isaiah 53?
  • How did the Methodist Discipline handle church trials in 1884?

It searches by meaning, so a passage you half remember can still be found.

Credits and licences

Many texts come from the Christian Classics Ethereal Library at Calvin University, which asks that its files not be sold. This library is free and sells nothing. Others come from the Text Creation Partnership (EEBO-TCP, Evans-TCP and ECCO-TCP), the Internet Archive, Project Gutenberg and Standard Ebooks. Every book's file names the page it came from.

The Greek and Latin texts of the Church Fathers and Philo, and Yonge's English translation of Philo, come from the Open Greek and Latin Project at the University of Leipzig, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. They were converted from TEI XML to Markdown, and the library's copies are shared under the same license.
