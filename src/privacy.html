What happens to your question

When your AI app uses the library, your question passes through a few hands. Here is each one, what it sees, and what it keeps.

Last checked 11 November 2026.

In short

  • The connector keeps no copy of your conversations or documents, and it doesn't log what you search for.
  • It never sees your whole conversation or your files.
  • Two outside services are involved: WorkOS for sign-in and Voyage AI for search. Cloudflare carries the traffic.
  • Your AI app holds the conversation itself, under its own privacy terms.
  • No ads, analytics, cookies or tracking, on the connector or this website. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Who sees what

WhoSeesKeeps
Your AI app (Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini)The whole conversation.Whatever its own privacy terms say. It is the largest holder of your data, and the library has no say over it.
CloudflareThe requests your AI app sends to the connector, in transit.Its own traffic records, under Cloudflare's terms.
WorkOS (sign-in)Your email address or GitHub login, and when you signed in.Your sign-in account. I can see the list of people who have signed in, and use it to count sign-ups and, if needed, block abuse.
The connector (a computer I run)The search your AI app sends: usually a short query it writes from your question, not the conversation.No search text. See below.
Voyage AI (search)The search words, and the library passages being ranked against them.Nothing. The library's Voyage account is opted out of data use, so Voyage deletes the text once it has processed it and doesn't train on it.

What the connector records

  • A log line for each request: which tool was used, whether it worked, how long it took, and a made-up ID for the person (part of a hash of the sign-in ID, not your email address). Not the words you searched for.
  • Counts for the daily limit: how many requests each ID made today and this minute. Cleared after a week.
  • Monthly totals: how much searching cost, not tied to any person.

A troubleshooting switch could add search text to the log. It is off.

What it never receives

  • Your conversation. The AI app sends only the search it wants run.
  • Your files. The connector can't read anything on your device or in your AI app.
  • Your IP address or location. Requests normally come from your AI app's servers, not from you, and the server keeps no access log. If you connect a tool that runs on your own computer (Claude Code, for example), Cloudflare sees that computer's IP address as any website would.

Email and public forms

Email to hello@christianopenlibrary.org is read only by me. The library's forms on GitHub are public, so leave out anything private if you use them.

Check it yourself
