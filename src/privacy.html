What happens to your question

Your question passes through a few hands. Here is what each one sees and keeps.

Last checked 20 November 2026.

In short

  • The connector keeps no copy of your conversations or documents. It doesn't log what you search for.
  • It never sees your whole conversation or your files.
  • Two outside services are involved: WorkOS for sign-in and Voyage AI for search. Cloudflare carries the traffic.
  • Your AI app holds the conversation itself, under its own privacy terms.
  • No ads, analytics, cookies or tracking on the connector or this website. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

What the connector records

  • A log line for each request: which tool was used, whether it worked and how long it took. It notes whether the answer found a match or said nothing was found. It also has a made-up ID for the person. The ID is part of a hash of the sign-in ID, not your email address. The words you searched for are not logged.
  • Counts for the daily limits: how many requests each ID made today and this minute, and how many passages it read in full today. Not which passages. Cleared after a week.
  • Monthly totals: how much searching cost. These aren't tied to any person.
  • Daily counts of searches that found nothing: how many answers said nothing was found. Also how many searches came within two minutes of the same person's last one. We use them to see where the library falls short. They are numbers only, with no IDs and no search words.

A troubleshooting switch could add search text to the log. It is off.

What it never receives

  • Your conversation. The AI app sends only the search it wants run.
  • Your files. The connector can't read anything on your device or in your AI app.
  • Your IP address or location. Requests normally come from your AI app's servers, not from you. The server keeps no access log. If you connect a tool that runs on your own computer (Claude Code, for example), Cloudflare sees that computer's IP address as any website would.

The search box on this website

  • What you type goes to the library's server and to Voyage AI, which ranks the passages. Cloudflare carries it. Neither keeps a record of the words.
  • On the Browse page, the list of authors and titles is searched in your browser. That sends nothing. If your words match no author or title, they are searched in the books once you stop typing. It's as if you had pressed Enter.
  • The server keeps each answer for a day so a shared link opens quickly. Each answer is sealed. Only someone searching the same words can open it.
  • A shared link carries the search inside the link itself, after the #. This website never receives it.
  • Your IP address is used only to count searches for the daily limit. It is kept as a scrambled code that changes every day and is deleted the next day.
  • No sign-in, cookies or tracking.

Email and public forms

Email to hello@christianopenlibrary.org is read by the library's staff. An AI assistant (Claude) sorts new mail first. Nothing in it is shared or used for anything else. The library's forms on GitHub are public. Leave out anything private if you use them.
