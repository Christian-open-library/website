Answers from your own documents

Your people already ask AI about your polity and policies. Let it answer from what you actually adopted.

  • It quotes only documents you approve, word for word, with the page.
  • You say which edition is in force. Older editions stay, labeled as historical.
  • New amendments, minutes and reports go in after each assembly, synod or board meeting.
  • Every answer links to your official copy and says it is not an official ruling.
  • When your documents don't answer a question, it says so.

Who sees what

Each document is set to one of three levels. Restricted documents never appear in anyone else's results.

LevelFor example
EveryoneConstitution, book of order, position papers, published minutes
Your membersDirectories, handbooks, committee reports
People you nameClosed minutes, judicial and personnel records

You get your own connector address, under your name, and can add or remove a person or a document at any time.

How your documents are kept private

If you are weighing whether to share closed minutes, here is how they are cared for.

  • Questions aren't recordedThe connector keeps no record of what anyone asks.
  • Not used to train AI, and not soldVoyage AI, which ranks passages, deletes them right away and doesn't train on them.
  • Encrypted on the wayEvery request between the AI app and our server.
  • Open for you to checkOur code, security review and privacy notes are public.
  • Yours to take backAsk, and we remove your documents and delete our copy.

How we start

  1. We build a working version on your public documents, at no cost.
  2. Your staff try it and tell us what to change.
  3. We add your restricted documents and set who sees them.
  4. Your people add one address in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Grok, on phone or computer, and sign in with Google or email. There is no software to install and nothing to train staff on.

You pay only for our work on your own documents.

The open library comes with it

Your documents sit beside the Christian Open Library: about 7,700 books, from the Church Fathers, Reformers and Puritans to Edwards and Spurgeon, with the historic creeds, commentaries, Greek and Hebrew lexicons, Bible translations, and the historic records of more than 80 denominations, including the bodies yours came from. Every text is public domain or used by permission, and none is written or translated by AI. It stays free to everyone and is never part of the price.

Questions

Will an AI company train on our minutes?

We don't, and Voyage AI has data use turned off. A passage someone reads in their AI app stays in that conversation under the app's own terms. The business plans of Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini don't train on conversations by default.

Can people outside our organization see restricted documents?

No. Restricted documents are served only to the accounts you approve.

Is an answer an official ruling?

No. Every answer quotes your documents and links to your official copy. Interpretation stays with your courts, boards and officers.

Where is it hosted?

On a server we run, behind Cloudflare. The connector keeps no copy of anyone's conversations.

What happens if we leave?

We remove your documents from the connector and delete our copy.

Who runs it?

The Christian Open Library, an independent library founded by Matt Miller, a pastor in the Presbyterian Church in America. It is not a ministry of any church or denomination, and its code is public.
