One source of record

Your people already ask AI about your polity and policies. Take the guesswork out of what your documents actually say.

  • Answers come only from documents you approve, quoted word for word with the page.
  • You set which edition is in force. Earlier editions stay available, labeled as historical.
  • New amendments, minutes and reports are added after each assembly, synod or board meeting.
  • Every answer links to your official copy and is marked as not an official ruling.
  • When your documents don't answer a question, it says so.

  • Set each document to public, members only, or the officers, staff and committees you name.

Questions

Will an AI company train on our minutes?

We don't, and Voyage AI has data use turned off. A passage someone reads in their AI app stays in that conversation under the app's own terms. The business plans of Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini don't train on conversations by default.

Can people outside our organization see restricted papers?

No. Restricted papers are served only to the accounts you approve.

Is an answer an official ruling?

No. Every answer quotes your documents and links to your official copy. Interpretation stays with your courts, boards and officers.

Where is it hosted?

On a server we run, behind Cloudflare. The connector keeps no copy of anyone's conversations.

What happens if we leave?

We remove your documents from the connector and delete our copy.

Who runs it?

Matt Miller, a pastor in the Presbyterian Church in America. The library is his personal project, and its code is public.
