How we start

  1. We build a working version from your public documents at no cost.
  2. Your staff try it and tell us what to change.
  3. We add your restricted documents and set who sees them.
  4. Your people add one address in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Grok on their phone or computer. They sign in with Google or email. There is no software to install and nothing to train staff on.

You pay only for our work on your own documents. That means a set-up fee and then a yearly fee. You get the quote before you decide.

Questions

Will an AI company train on our minutes?

We don't. Voyage AI has data use turned off. A passage someone reads in their AI app stays in that conversation under the app's own terms. The business plans of Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini don't train on conversations by default.

Can people outside our organization see restricted documents?

No. Restricted documents are served only to the accounts you approve.

Is an answer an official ruling?

No. Every answer quotes your documents and links to your official copy. Interpretation stays with your courts, boards and officers.

Where is it hosted?

On a server we run, behind Cloudflare. The connector keeps no copy of anyone's conversations.

What happens if we leave?

We remove your documents from the connector and delete our copy.

Who runs it?

The Christian Open Library. It is an independent library founded by Matt Miller, a pastor in the Presbyterian Church in America. It is not a ministry of any church or denomination. Its code is public.
