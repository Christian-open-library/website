Set it up

It takes about five minutes. You paste one address into your AI app's settings, then sign in with your email. You do not need a GitHub account.

The address

You will paste this into your AI app:

https://mcp.christianopenlibrary.org/mcp

Choose your AI app

  • Claude
  • ChatGPT
  • Gemini

Menu names change from time to time. If you don't see the exact words below, look in your app's settings for "connectors" or "MCP". If you get stuck, write to hello@christianopenlibrary.org and tell me which app you use.

Claude

  1. Open Claude and go to Settings.
  2. Choose Connectors.
  3. Choose Add custom connector.
  4. Paste the address above.
  5. Choose Connect, then sign in.

ChatGPT

This needs a paid ChatGPT plan.

  1. Open ChatGPT and go to Settings.
  2. Choose Apps & Connectors.
  3. Turn on Developer mode.
  4. Choose Create.
  5. Paste the address above and choose OAuth as the sign-in method.
  6. Save it, then sign in.

Gemini

  1. Open Gemini and go to Settings.
  2. Choose Connected Apps.
  3. Choose Add a custom app.
  4. Paste the address above, then sign in.

Signing in

A sign-in page opens. Type your email address and I'll send you a short code. Type the code in, and you're connected. You can sign in with GitHub instead if you already have an account, but you don't need one.

Sign-in is handled by an outside service, WorkOS. What happens to your question says exactly who sees what.

Then try it

Start a new conversation and ask something. You may need to switch the connector on for that conversation (look for a tools or connectors button near the message box). Some questions to try:

  • Search the Christian Open Library: what did Calvin teach about the Lord's Supper?
  • What did Spurgeon say about Psalm 23?
  • Which historic writers cited Isaiah 53?
  • Find a passage about assurance of salvation in the Puritans.

Naming the library in your first question helps your app know to use it.

Good to know

  • Each person has a fair-use daily limit.
  • Your app writes the answer. The library finds and quotes the passages. Check a quotation against its source before you print or preach it. How to check it.
  • If you remember only the gist, try two or three phrasings, or one distinctive phrase.

Questions, corrections or a book that is missing? Write to hello@christianopenlibrary.org.
