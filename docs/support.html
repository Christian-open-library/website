What it costs this month

Updated every night from the actual bills and gifts.

What the money goes to

  • Searches: each question is matched by meaning through Voyage AI, which charges by the amount of text it reads. This grows with use.
  • Adding new books: every new book is read once by Voyage AI so it can be searched.
  • The server: a small computer that runs around the clock, and its electricity.
  • The domain name: christianopenlibrary.org.

The time spent finding, checking and adding books isn't on the card.

How to give

  • Buy Me a Coffee: monthly or once, by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. No account needed.
  • GitHub Sponsors: monthly or once, by card, if you have a GitHub account.
  • Bitcoin: send over Lightning to mattm4444@strike.me (paste it into your wallet's send field).

Sponsor a month

A person, a class or a church can give one month's costs, shown on the card above. If you'd like to be thanked by name on the card for that month, say so in your gift's message, or write to hello@christianopenlibrary.org. Otherwise every gift stays anonymous.

The library is not yet a registered charity or under a fiscal sponsor, so gifts are received by its founder, Matt Miller, and are not tax-deductible. It is not a ministry of any church. Giving is never needed to use it.
