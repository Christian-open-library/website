Your official source in every AI app

A private connector for denominations, churches, seminaries and ministries. Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Grok answer from your constitution, policies, handbooks and minutes, not from memory or the open web.

Talk to us How we protect your documents

One source of record

Your people already ask AI about your polity and policies. Take the guesswork out of what your documents actually say.

  • Answers come only from documents you approve, quoted word for word with the page.
  • You set which edition is in force. Earlier editions stay available, labeled as historical.
  • New amendments, minutes and reports are added after each assembly, synod or board meeting.
  • Every answer links to your official copy and is marked as not an official ruling.
  • When your documents don't answer a question, it says so.

Access you control

Publish to everyone, or keep documents to the people who need them.

  • Set each document to public, members only, or the officers, staff and committees you name.
  • Restricted documents never appear in anyone else's results.
  • Your own connector address, under your name.
  • Add or remove a person or a document at any time.
  • A denomination's records sit beside the historic records of the bodies it came from, and about 7,700 Christian books.

Confidential by design

Built so a stated clerk, registrar or general counsel can hand over closed minutes with confidence.

  • No query logging. The connector doesn't record what anyone asks.
  • Never used to train AI, never sold. Voyage AI, which ranks passages, deletes them straight away and doesn't train on them.
  • Encrypted in transit. Every request between the AI app and our server.
  • Open to inspection. Our code, security review and privacy notes are public.
  • Yours to withdraw. Ask, and we remove your documents and delete our copy.

Nothing to deploy

It works where your people already are.

  • Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Grok, on phone or computer.
  • People add one address and sign in with Google or email.
  • No software, servers or training for your staff.

How we start

We build a working version on your public documents at no cost. Your staff review it; then we add restricted documents and set who sees them. You pay only for our work on your documents. The public-domain library stays free for everyone.

Questions

Will an AI company train on our minutes?

We don't, and Voyage AI has data use turned off. A passage someone reads in their AI app stays in that conversation under the app's own terms. The business plans of Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini don't train on conversations by default.

Can people outside our organization see restricted documents?

No. Restricted documents are served only to the accounts you approve.

Is an answer an official ruling?

No. Every answer quotes your documents and links to your official copy. Interpretation stays with your courts, boards and officers.

Where is it hosted?

On a server we run, behind Cloudflare. The connector keeps no copy of anyone's conversations.

What happens if we leave?

We remove your documents from the connector and delete our copy.

Who runs it?

The Christian Open Library, an independent library founded by Matt Miller, a pastor in the Presbyterian Church in America. It is not a ministry of any church or denomination, and its code is public.
