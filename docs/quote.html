Check a quote

Searches the text of 7,100 books. Searches aren’t logged.

Give your AI app the books themselves. Connect the library to Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Grok. It then searches all 7,100 works and quotes the passage with its source, not from memory. Free.

How to read the results

The answer comes from the books: your words are compared with their text, word by word. No AI writes it. The words found are marked in the passage; words that differ are underlined.

  • Found in the Bible. The words are a Bible verse, word for word in one of the library's seven translations (the King James Version first). The result gives the reference, then the works that quote it.
  • Close to a Bible verse. No translation in the library has the words exactly, but most of them are in one verse, in the same order. The wording may be from a translation the library does not hold.
  • Verified word for word. The words are in the work named, as submitted. Spelling, capitals and punctuation are not compared.
  • Found with different wording. The passage is in the work, but the quotation has changed in circulation. The result shows the wording in the source.
  • Not found in the works of the author named. The words are in the library, but in none of its works by that author. The result gives the earliest work that has them.
  • Found only as quoted by another writer. The earliest work with the words is by someone else, who gives them as the author's. The author may have written them in a work the library does not hold.
  • Not found in the library. No work in the library has the words or a close match.
Limits of this search
  • “Not found” does not mean false. The library holds about 7,100 works, not everything ever written. Most books published after 1930, such as those of C. S. Lewis, are still in copyright and are not included.
  • The earliest work in the library is not always the origin. A saying can be older than any book the library holds.
  • Scanned texts contain misread letters. Where a text was read by computer from a scan, compare the printed page before quoting it.
  • The checker can be wrong. If an answer looks wrong, use Think this answer is wrong? Tell me under it. Fixes are listed in the corrections log.

See also Accuracy

Privacy

The words you check go to the library's server and to Voyage AI, whose search index helps locate reworded passages. Cloudflare carries them. Neither keeps a record of them. So that a shared result opens quickly, the server keeps each answer for a day, sealed so that only someone checking the same words can open it. A share link carries the quotation inside the link itself, after the #, so this website never receives it. Your IP address is used only to count checks for the daily limit, as a scrambled code that changes every day and is deleted the next day. Details.
