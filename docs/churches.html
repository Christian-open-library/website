For churches

Your people already ask ChatGPT and Claude about your book of church order, and those apps often answer from memory. We can make them answer from your own documents.

Write to us

The URCNA's Church Order, Acts of Synod and study committee reports are in the library now.

What we set up

  • Your current editionYou say which edition is current, and we add each new one after your assembly or synod.
  • Your own addressOne that searches only your documents, to give your officers or members. Papers you don't publish can be kept to the people you choose.
  • Marked as yoursEvery quotation says "Used by permission" and links to your official copy. It is never offered as a ruling.

Anyone who names your church also gets the historic records of the bodies it came from, which are already in the library.

What it costs

The public-domain books stay free for everyone and are never sold. A church pays only for our work on its own documents. We'll build a working version on yours first, at no cost, so you can try it before deciding.

Write to us

We don't keep anyone's searches, so there is never a report of who asked what.
