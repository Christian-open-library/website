For churches, denominations and organizations

We put your constitution, minutes, reports and papers beside about 7,700 Christian books and the records of more than 80 denominations. Your people study them from Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Grok.

Write to us

Ask how a rule changed, what an assembly or board decided, or for every report on a subject, and it answers from the documents themselves. There's nothing to install: they add one address to the app they already use.

Every quotation can be checked

Quotations are word for word, with the page and a link to the source. When your documents don't say, it tells you so. Our test results are public, misses and all.

Who sees what

We don't keep what anyone asks. Papers you don't publish can be kept to the people you choose.

What it costs

The public-domain books stay free for everyone. You pay only for our work on your documents: loading them, adding each new edition or set of minutes, and your own address. We'll build a working version on yours first, at no cost.
