For churches

Your constitution, minutes and reports, and the records of every body you came from, ready for deep research in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Grok.

Write to us

One question, answered from two editions of the same rule, forty years apart. Your current documents sit beside the old ones.

What your people get

  • Deep researchIt reads across your church order, minutes and reports, and back through the records of the bodies you came from.
  • Real analysisTrace how a rule changed, compare what assemblies decided, or gather every report on a subject, in one question.
  • AccuracyEvery quotation is word for word, with the page and a link to the source. When your documents don't say, it says so. Test results.
  • PrivacyWe don't keep what anyone asks. Papers you don't publish can be kept to the people you choose.
  • EaseIt works inside the AI app they already use. Add one address and ask in plain words.

What it costs

The public-domain books stay free for everyone and are never sold. A church pays only for our work on its documents: loading them, adding each new edition after your assembly or synod, and an address of your own. We'll build a working version on yours first, at no cost.
