Thank you for writing, and for your interest in the library.

Apart from what a book's own open license allows, the library's books can't be used in apps, websites or AI services built on the collection (more than 10 books), for AI training, or in anything paid, and I don't make exceptions on request. The rules are on christianopenlibrary.org/download.html#reuse.

The original texts are free at their own sources, such as the Christian Classics Ethereal Library (ccel.org), Internet Archive (archive.org), EEBO-TCP and Project Gutenberg, and you're welcome to use them there on those sites' own terms. For your own study you're welcome to the library itself: christianopenlibrary.org shows how to sign in to the AI connector or ask for access to the books.
