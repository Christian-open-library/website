Thanks for asking. I've invited you to the library for your own reading and study: accept the invitation from GitHub's email or at https://github.com/orgs/Christian-open-library/invitation.

The library's books can't be used in an app, website or AI service, for AI training, or in anything paid, and I don't make exceptions on request. The rules: https://christianopenlibrary.org/download.html#reuse

The originals are free at their own sources (each file names its source: CCEL, Internet Archive, EEBO-TCP, Project Gutenberg and others), so you can use them there, on those sites' own terms.
